Reinhart Sulilatu

I write smart contracts and the off-chain services that keep them honest. Most of my work is on EVM chains: escrow, verifiable randomness, settlement, and the keepers that push a stalled protocol to its next state.

The backend half is the half I like. The contract is usually the smallest piece of the system; the resolution keeper, the indexer, and the failure paths around it are where the time actually goes.

Lotix is live on Robinhood Chain and BSC: two deployments on two chains, both driving the same accounting engine.
